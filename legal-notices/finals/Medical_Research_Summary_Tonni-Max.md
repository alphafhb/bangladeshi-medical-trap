## 6. Causation — stated plainly, competing explanations addressed

**The case:** a healthy normotensive non-diabetic 26-year-old suffers immediate violent seizures upon spinal anesthesia, is held ~2 hours without ICU care, arrives with SpO₂ <28% and fixed pupils, and has bilateral hemorrhagic brain injury on MRI at day 19 progressing to end-stage cortical atrophy. The treating ICU itself raised drug hypersensitivity. The pre-anesthetic sheet is blank and the operation note unsigned. This is breach + causation on contemporaneous third-party paper.

**Competing explanations, and why they fail or need work:**
- **"Eclampsia"** (Dr. Hariprakash Chakravarthy's note, Mar 2024): eclampsia without hypertension is medically incoherent — she was **120/80 at admission, normotensive throughout** the contemporaneous record, and non-diabetic 13 days prior. NINS, the apex institute, diagnosed "Hypoxic Encephalopathy" with no eclampsia mention. The label must be reconciled, not ignored — the defense will cite it.
- **"MSA-C"** (Nov 2024 MRI): the reporting radiologist attributes it as **possible HIE sequelae**; a degenerative disease does not begin with SpO₂ <28% and fixed pupils. Keep the causal framing primary.
- **Rivotril/asthma history:** disclose in the brief; neither causes post-spinal convulsions with anoxic brain injury.
- **Severe hypocalcemia (5.5, Aug 2023):** drawn 2.5 weeks AFTER the event — cannot explain immediate post-injection seizures. Do not let the defense muddy the timeline.
