## A. Constitutional — the writ route (High Court Division)
| Provision | What it does | Use in this case |
|---|---|---|
| **Art. 32** — Constitution | Right to life & personal liberty | Her core right, destroyed by negligence |
| **Art. 31** | Right to protection of law | State's failure to regulate/facilities |
| **Art. 27** | Equality before law | Supporting ground |
| **Art. 44 + Art. 102** | Right to move the HCD; writ jurisdiction | **The vehicle** — compensation via constitutional tort (*Jihad*, *Russel Sarker*) |
| Art. 15(a), 18(1) | Directive principles: healthcare a basic necessity; public health a state duty | Moral/legal backdrop (non-justiciable, often cited) |

## B. Civil suit for damages (tort)
| Provision | What it does | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **CPC 1908, s.9** | Civil courts' jurisdiction over suits of civil nature | Forum for itemized damages (*Cathy Masud* route) |
| **CPC, Order VII** | Plaint requirements (facts, cause of action, valuation) | Drafting the suit |
| **CPC, Order XXXII, Rule 15** | Suits for persons of **unsound mind** via **next friend** | Parents sue as Tonni's next friend — she is legally incapacitated |
| **Limitation Act 1908, First Schedule, Art. 22** | Compensation for injury to the person: **1 year** from when the injury is committed | ⚠️ **URGENT:** incident Jul 2023 — prima facie time-barred |
| **Limitation Act, s.6** | Legal disability: if the person entitled is of **unsound mind**, the period runs only after disability ceases | **The saving argument** — her incapacity suspends the clock; counsel to plead |
| **Limitation Act, s.23** | Continuing wrong: fresh period at every moment the wrong continues | Supporting argument (ongoing injury) |

### The three provisions that decide this case's shape
1. **Art. 102** — the fast vehicle (no court fee, private-clinic precedent, compensation Tk 9L–1cr observed).
2. **Limitation Act Art. 22 + s.6** — the civil suit lives or dies on the disability argument. **Counsel's first job.**
3. **Penal Code s.338 + BMDC Act s.23** — the criminal/disciplinary threat stack that makes settlement rational for her.
