## Cross-cutting observations

**Clearest pre/post contrasts:**
1. **Body habitus:** healthy, normal-weight young woman (pre) → extreme emaciation with visible bony contours of arms, shoulders, clavicles, temples (post).
2. **Function:** standing, smiling, feeding cake to others, posing for photos (pre) → permanently bedridden, NG-tube fed (post).
3. **Devices:** none (pre) → NG tube in all 14 post files; tracheostomy tube directly visible in 3 video frames, probable tracheostomy dressing in 1 home photo (post).
4. **Hair:** full head of styled hair (pre) → buzzed/shaved very short (post).
5. **Eyes:** engaged, camera-directed gaze and smile (pre) → eyes open in nearly all post files but gaze directed upward/laterally, never fixing on the camera (observed, not diagnosed).

**Devices NOT visible in any frame:** VP shunt (no clear shunt tract or reservoir visible — scalp views are limited), urinary catheter (not visible in any frame).

**Flags for a clinician's eyes:**
- `video2_frame01/02/03.jpg` — peristomal skin condition around the tracheostomy; also the character of the eye gaze.
- `WhatsApp Image 2026-10-19 at 17.24.09.jpeg` — the orange wrist wrap: restraint vs. dressing, and skin integrity beneath.
- `WhatsApp Image 2026-10-19 at 17.35.37.jpeg` — the white neck gauze: confirm it is the tracheostomy dressing and assess change status/skin.
- General: no frame shows the VP shunt site or the urinary catheter — dedicated photos of both would strengthen the visual record.
