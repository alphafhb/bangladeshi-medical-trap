## 1. Executive Clinical Summary

On **July 24, 2023**, Ms. Sanzida Khatun (Tonni), a previously healthy 26-year-old university student with no baseline hypertension or diabetes, was admitted to Motherland Infertility Center and Hospital, Rajshahi, for a planned Cesarean delivery (LUCS). 

During induction of anesthesia at ~4:00 PM, she was administered a Subarachnoid Block (spinal anesthesia). Immediately following injection, she suffered an acute catastrophic collapse characterized by violent lower extremity and generalized convulsions, cardiac ischemia/NSTEMI, malignant hypertension ($180/120\text{ mmHg}$), and profound respiratory failure. 

Instead of an immediate emergency transfer to a tertiary ICU within 10–15 minutes, **the clinic retained the convulsing, suffocating patient for nearly two hours**, frantically administering intravenous loop diuretics (Lasix) and beta-blockers (Labecard/Labetalol) to battle acute pulmonary edema and hypertension in an unequipped clinic. By the time she reached Islami Bank Medical College Hospital (IBMCH) ICU, her oxygen saturation was **below 28%** and her pupils were **bilaterally fixed, dilated, and unreactive**, signifying tentorial brain herniation and catastrophic global cerebral anoxia.

Over the succeeding three years (2023–2026), the prolonged lack of oxygen resulted in **severe Hypoxic-Ischemic Encephalopathy (HIE)**, diffuse laminar cortical necrosis, secondary communicating hydrocephalus requiring neurosurgery at the **National Institute of Neurosciences & Hospital (NINS), Dhaka** for a **Ventriculoperitoneal (VP) Shunt on January 18, 2025**, chronic tracheostomy dependence, chronic catheter-induced hydronephrosis, and extreme neurogenic muscle wasting, causing her weight to collapse from **50 kg down to 24 kg**.
